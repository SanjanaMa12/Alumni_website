Message from Alumni Relationship Office

Welcome to the Alumni Family!

We are truly delighted to welcome you back to your alma mater — the University of Peradeniya, a place where lifelong friendships are formed, knowledge is shared, and memories are created.

The Alumni Relations Office (ARO) is dedicated to nurturing a lifelong and meaningful connection between the University and its diverse alumni community across Sri Lanka and around the world. Through the Alumni Relations Office (ARO), we provide a platform for you to reconnect with your fellow Peradeniya alumni, faculty, juniors, and the wider university community.

As a member of our alumni family, you can contribute by:

• Mentor and guide the next generation of students.

• Support scholarships and student initiatives.

• Contribute to research and development.

• Share career opportunities and internships.

• Continue to be part of the University’s growth and progress.

The ARO portal also brings you closer to the traditions and experiences that make Peradeniya special — from our Annual Open Day, Drama Festivals, and Faculty Reunions to new opportunities for alumni to connect, collaborate, and give back.

Whether you graduated recently or many years ago, Peradeniya will always be a part of your story. Once a Peradeniyan, always a Peradeniyan.
